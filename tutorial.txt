============================================================
TestBox — User Tutorial
============================================================

Welcome! This guide walks a new user through TestBox from the
first launch to cloud sync and mobile usage. Follow the sections
in order the first time; afterwards, jump to whatever you need.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. What is TestBox?
------------------------------------------------------------

TestBox is an offline-first exam workbook. It helps you:

* Organize your courses into folders and subjects.
* Build practice exams from your own questions.
* Answer with an optional timer or stopwatch.
* Review results, statistics, notes, and marked questions.
* Keep everything on your device — and optionally sync it to
  the cloud across Windows, Android, and the web.

Everything works without an internet connection. An account is
only needed for cloud sync.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. First launch
------------------------------------------------------------

When you open TestBox you land on the Home page. The layout is:

* Top bar — search, sync status, account, and weather (mobile).
* Sidebar (desktop) or bottom navigation (mobile) — Home,
  Folders, Subjects, Tags, Calendar, Results, Settings.
* Main area — the current page.

Your data is created locally the moment you start. No account,
no wizard, no waiting.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. Creating an account
------------------------------------------------------------

Accounts are optional and only used for cloud sync.

1. Open the menu and choose Sign up (ساخت حساب).
2. Enter your email and a password (at least 6 characters).
3. Submit. The account is confirmed automatically — no
   verification email is required.
4. You return to the app already signed in.

Your local data stays where it is; syncing begins when you use
the sync button (see section 20).

------------------------------------------------------------
4. Login
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Open Sign in (ورود).
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Submit.

If you are already signed in, the app keeps you signed in
across restarts — a restored login link or deep link will not
log you out. Signing out is done from Settings → "Sign out"
(خروج از حساب).

------------------------------------------------------------
5. Creating folders
------------------------------------------------------------

Folders are your top-level organization (one per course, for
example).

1. Go to Folders.
2. Press "New folder" (فولدر جدید).
3. Type a name.
4. Optionally attach subjects to it now (section 6).
5. Save.

Folders appear in the list. Open one to see and manage its
exams.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. Adding subjects
------------------------------------------------------------

Subjects are labels like "Math" or "Physics" that you attach to
folders and filter by.

1. Go to Subjects.
2. Press "New subject" (درس جدید).
3. Type a name and save.

To attach subjects to a folder, open the folder's subject
editor (from the folder card, or the chip button on a list row)
and tap the subject chips. A folder can have several subjects;
the first one is its primary subject. Changes sync with your
account.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. Creating exams
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Open a folder.
2. Press the new-exam button.
3. Enter the exam name and choose its type.
4. Save.

The exam card now appears in the folder. Use the folder's sort
control to order exams by name, date, or your own manual order.

------------------------------------------------------------
8. Creating practice sessions
------------------------------------------------------------

Practice mode lets you answer questions at your own pace with
immediate scoring:

1. Open an exam.
2. Choose practice mode.
3. Answer question by question; save answers as you go.

You can stop at any time — answers are stored locally and
resumed when you return.

------------------------------------------------------------
9. Answering questions
------------------------------------------------------------

For each question you can:

* Pick an option or type a free-text answer.
* Add a note (your private comment for the question).
* Mark it for later review (flag icon) — marked questions are
  collected on the Marked page.
* Attach tags for your own classification.

Answers save automatically; there is no "submit" step inside a
practice session.

------------------------------------------------------------
10. Timer
------------------------------------------------------------

Exams can run with a countdown timer:

1. When starting a timed exam, set the duration (or use the
   exam's saved duration).
2. The timer shows remaining time in the focus bar.
3. At zero the exam can auto-finish according to its settings.

Stopwatch mode is also available for untimed practice, and
study-time statistics accumulate from your sessions.

------------------------------------------------------------
11. Pages navigation
------------------------------------------------------------

Long exams are split into pages (100 questions per page):

* Use the page arrows to move between pages.
* For very large exams (1000+ questions), open the Pages
  navigator (grid icon in the focus bar) to jump to any page in
  one click; the current page is highlighted.

------------------------------------------------------------
12. Jumping to the last answered question
------------------------------------------------------------

On each exam card, the clock button deep-links to the highest
question you have already answered, so a large exam resumes
where you left off. The button is disabled when nothing is
answered yet.

------------------------------------------------------------
13. Notes
------------------------------------------------------------

Every question has a notes field. Notes are saved with the
question, sync to the cloud, and are visible while answering
and during review. Use them for explanations, mnemonics, or
reminders.

------------------------------------------------------------
14. Marked questions
------------------------------------------------------------

Flag any question while answering. The Marked page collects all
flagged questions from an exam (or across exams) so you can
review exactly the ones you were unsure about.

------------------------------------------------------------
15. Results
------------------------------------------------------------

After finishing an exam, the Results page shows:

* Score according to the exam's scoring scheme (custom
  numbering, partial answer keys, negative marking).
* Correct / wrong / unanswered / unresolved counts.
* A question-by-question review with your answers, notes, and
  tags.

Statistics pages add study-time and activity trends over days.

------------------------------------------------------------
16. Sorting folders
------------------------------------------------------------

On the Folders page, the sort dropdown offers:

* Name (Persian/numeric aware), Created, Updated.
* Custom — your own manual order (section 18).

The choice is remembered per user across sessions.

------------------------------------------------------------
17. Card/List view
------------------------------------------------------------

Next to the sort dropdown, switch between:

* List view — compact rows, fastest for many folders (default).
* Card view — a grid with subject chips and richer details.

The view persists across reloads. Long folder names in card
view wrap over up to three lines instead of being cut off.

------------------------------------------------------------
18. Manual ordering
------------------------------------------------------------

Manual order works for both folders and exams and is protected
by an explicit edit mode:

1. Choose sort = Custom.
2. Press "Edit order" (ویرایش ترتیب). Move items with the
   arrow buttons (or drag in list view).
3. Press "Save order" (ذخیره ترتیب) to apply — the order is
   stored and becomes your custom order.
4. Or press Cancel (لغو) to discard all changes; the previous
   order stays exactly as it was.

While edit mode is on, other toolbar actions (sort/view
switching) are locked so your reorder session is never
accidentally interrupted. Leaving the page without saving also
discards unsaved changes.

The same flow exists for exams inside a folder (per-folder exam
order).

------------------------------------------------------------
19. Backup / export JSON
------------------------------------------------------------

Settings → Backup → Export downloads your entire dataset as a
JSON file:

* Web/Windows: dated file testbox-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json.
* Android: the system file picker opens (SAF); choose a
  location and the JSON is written there. Cancelling the picker
  cancels the export — nothing is reported as saved.

Keep exported files somewhere safe; they are your full local
copy.

------------------------------------------------------------
20. Sync between devices
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Sign in with the same account on each device.
2. Press the sync button in the top bar (or wait for the
   60-second automatic heartbeat).
3. Status shows "syncing" then "saved" (ذخیره شد).

Sync uploads your local changes first, then downloads cloud
changes — folders, their full subject lists, subjects, exams,
answers, tags, settings, and daily activity. Editing on device
A and signing in on device B shows the same data after one
sync. Data from other accounts is never visible to you (each
account is isolated).

------------------------------------------------------------
21. Offline usage
------------------------------------------------------------

No connection? Keep working: creating and editing folders,
subjects, exams, answers, notes, and tags all continue locally.
An offline prompt tells you the state of the connection; the
moment you are back online, queued changes upload on the next
sync. Sign-in-related actions wait until you are online.

------------------------------------------------------------
22. Settings
------------------------------------------------------------

Settings includes:

* Account — email, sign in/out.
* Backup — export JSON (section 19).
* Sync — status and manual sync.
* Appearance — theme (section 23).
* Language — Persian/English (section 24).
* About — version and license.

------------------------------------------------------------
23. Theme
------------------------------------------------------------

Settings → Theme offers Light, Dark, or System. The choice is
applied instantly, remembered, and synchronized across open
windows/tabs. All pages, modals, and exam screens follow the
theme.

------------------------------------------------------------
24. Language switching
------------------------------------------------------------

Settings → Language switches the whole interface between
Persian (default) and English — menus, buttons, dialogs, and
toasts all change immediately. The choice persists across
restarts.

------------------------------------------------------------
25. Mobile usage
------------------------------------------------------------

On Android (and small screens):

* Bottom navigation replaces the sidebar.
* Modals stay above the keyboard; name inputs use a larger
  touch font so Android does not zoom.
* Persian typing in answers and notes works without resets.
* The hardware back button follows your history and only exits
  the app at the start page.
* Google sign-in opens in a native Custom Tab when available.
* JSON backup writes through the system file picker.
* The TopBar clears the status bar and keeps the weather
  visible.

That's everything you need to run TestBox day to day. Happy
studying — Learn · Practice · Grow.
